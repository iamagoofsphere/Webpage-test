Our Story

▲ our cat getting a wash
▲ our most popular cat
▲ our jolliest cat

Our little cafe started in 2019, we were just a family cafe with a love for cats. Our parents were baristas were 10 years of experience working in the food industry – and our siblings (including myself) started the cafe. The idea was simple; combine three things we love: cats, coffee and being cozy – and like that Cozy Cat Cafe was born. Each year, our team grew more. We started with 4 people, 5 cats in 2019 and we are now a team of 18 people and 24 cats in 2026. You can study, relax or just enjoy the food alongside cats. You can also play with the cats, pet them and even take them home if you want to adopt one. People loved the idea. One regular visitor once said, "These cats are friendly and abseloutely adorable!". Another said, "The cats really do bring that extra joy into my day". We have always dreamed of opening a cat-themed cafe and now, our dreams came true.

Our Popular Picks

▲ our most popular item

The Cat Latte is our most popular item. Brewed by our parents with 100% organic and whole milk and drawn by professionals. It's not just for show. It has a perfect balance of rich, bitter and sweet. It is crafted with our family's special recipe passed down from generations and generations. The coffee is made from top quality coffee beans and the milk comes from grass-fed, free-range cows. Our parents brew the latte and latte art professionals shape the milk into a beautiful cat. This will be unlike any other latte you have tasted. We are pawsitive.

▲ our newly added item

The Kitty Sandwich is our newest addition on our overgrowing menu. It contains lettuce, cheese, ham, etc. perfect for light snack. Which are all assembled to make a sandwich. Then, our team decorates the top part of the sandwith to make a kitty face! It is a light snack and one we think you will enjoy. This is my personal favourite because it looks so cute! Just look at it! You can't tell us that's cute!

▲ Catcakes!

Catcakes! This is our fan favourite. Imagine cupcakes but 1000 times jollier. You get catcakes! Not only this is delicous, it is also very cute. Not only do we have catcakes(cat cupcakes), we also have bunnycakes and pandacakes! What are you waiting for? Come and grab one!!!

You can choose from our menu full of high quality, mouth-watering deserts. Choose our coffee latte, The Cat Latte for a nice, hot drink or choose our delicous sandwich, cupcakes, drinks, etc. to get the best experience. Designed by our family and made by professional cooks. Don't forget to gently pet our cats too; They are always waiting for new visitors. Whether you just want to see, pet or stay with the cats or eat our food, we are sure you will enjoy the experience.
